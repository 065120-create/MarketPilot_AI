# Customer Journey Framework

## Overview
The Customer Journey Framework maps the complete lifecycle of a customer's interaction with a brand, from initial brand discovery to post-purchase advocacy. Understanding this journey allows marketers to orchestrate touchpoints, optimize conversion rates, and deliver a seamless omnichannel experience.

## Foundational Journey Models

### 1. The Traditional Funnel (AIDA)
- **Awareness:** The user realizes they have a problem and discovers your brand.
- **Interest:** The user researches solutions and shows interest in your offerings.
- **Desire:** The user develops a preference for your specific product/service.
- **Action:** The user makes a purchase or converts.

### 2. The See-Think-Do-Care Model (Google)
- **See:** Largest addressable qualified audience (Brand Awareness).
- **Think:** Audience showing weak commercial intent (Consideration).
- **Do:** Audience showing strong commercial intent (Conversion).
- **Care:** Current customers with two or more commercial transactions (Loyalty/Advocacy).

### 3. The Modern Lifecycle (Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Retention, Advocacy)
This model extends the traditional funnel to emphasize the critical post-purchase phases, acknowledging that acquiring a new customer is 5-25x more expensive than retaining an existing one.

## Journey Mapping and Orchestration

### Touchpoint Mapping
Identify every interaction a customer has with the brand across all channels (Ads, Website, Social Media, Email, Sales Reps, Customer Service).
- **Macro-conversions:** The ultimate goal (e.g., Purchase, Subscribing to software).
- **Micro-conversions:** Small steps that indicate intent and move the user forward (e.g., Newsletter signup, downloading a whitepaper, adding to cart, watching a demo video).

### Omnichannel Journey & Orchestration
Customers do not take a linear path. They may see a mobile ad, visit the site on a desktop, read an email, and purchase in-store.
- **Orchestration:** Using marketing automation and CDPs to deliver a unified, personalized experience across devices. If a user adds an item to their cart on mobile, they should see a retargeting ad on desktop, and receive an abandoned cart email.

## Measurement and Analysis

### Attribution Models
Attribution determines which touchpoints get credit for a conversion.
- **First-Touch:** Gives 100% credit to the first interaction (Good for understanding awareness drivers).
- **Last-Touch:** Gives 100% credit to the final interaction before conversion (Good for understanding bottom-funnel drivers).
- **Linear:** Gives equal credit to all touchpoints.
- **Time-Decay:** Gives more credit to touchpoints closer to the conversion time.
- **Position-Based (U-Shaped):** Gives 40% to first, 40% to last, and 20% distributed among the middle touchpoints.
- **Data-Driven / Algorithmic:** Uses machine learning to assign probabilistic weight to all touchpoints. (The most accurate, but requires high data volume).

### Drop-Off Analysis
Using analytics to identify where users abandon the journey.
- **Funnel Visualization:** Track the conversion rate between steps (e.g., Landing Page -> Cart -> Checkout -> Purchase).
- **Intervention:** If there is a massive drop-off between Cart and Checkout, investigate UX friction, unexpected shipping costs, or technical errors.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Treating the Journey as Linear:** Assuming customers flow perfectly from Step 1 to Step 5. In reality, journeys loop, stall, and jump across channels.
2. **Ignoring the "Care/Retention" Phase:** Funneling all budget into acquisition while allowing churn to destroy LTV.
3. **Internal Silos:** Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success operating independently, resulting in a disjointed experience for the customer.
4. **Over-relying on Last-Click Attribution:** This systematically undervalues top-of-funnel brand building and content marketing.

## Actionable Recommendations
- Conduct customer interviews to validate your assumed journey map against real customer experiences.
- Align content strategy with the journey: Educational blogs for 'Awareness', comparison guides for 'Consideration', and heavy discounts for 'Conversion'.
- Implement a Customer Data Platform (CDP) to stitch together cross-device identities and resolve attribution gaps.
- Monitor Time-to-Conversion (Sales Cycle Length). If the average journey takes 30 days, do not evaluate a new top-of-funnel campaign's ROI after 7 days.
