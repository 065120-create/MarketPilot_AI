# Content Strategy Framework

## Overview
A strong content strategy aligns business objectives with audience needs, ensuring that every piece of content drives measurable marketing outcomes. This framework covers content pillars, formats, calendar planning, distribution, and performance optimization across the funnel.

## Content Pillars and Ideation
Content pillars are 3-5 core themes that represent your brand's expertise and align with what your audience cares about.
- **Purpose:** Ensures consistency, prevents content drift, and builds SEO authority through cluster topics.
- **Example (B2B SaaS):** Pillar 1: Future of Work, Pillar 2: Productivity Hacks, Pillar 3: Product Use Cases, Pillar 4: Company Culture.
- **Storytelling Frameworks:** Use frameworks like the Hero's Journey (Customer is the hero, your brand is the guide/Yoda) or PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solution) to structure compelling narratives.

## Content Formats and Best Practices
Different channels require different formats. Adapting content natively to each platform is critical.
- **Short-Form Video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts):** Keep under 60 seconds. Hook the viewer in the first 3 seconds. Use native text overlays and trending audio.
- **Long-Form Video (YouTube):** Focus on educational value or deep entertainment. Optimize thumbnails and titles for high CTR.
- **Carousels (LinkedIn, Instagram):** High engagement format for step-by-step guides or data breakdowns.
- **User-Generated Content (UGC):** Authenticity drives conversion. Use customer testimonials, unboxings, and reviews, especially in paid social ads.
- **Blogs/SEO Content:** Focus on search intent. Use H2/H3 tags for readability, include internal links, and ensure thorough, original research.

## Content Mapping to the Funnel
Content must match the user's intent at their specific stage in the customer journey.
- **Top of Funnel (TOFU - Awareness):** Educational blog posts, viral short-form videos, infographics, PR. Goal: Maximize reach and traffic.
- **Middle of Funnel (MOFU - Consideration):** Case studies, webinars, email newsletters, comparison guides. Goal: Lead generation and nurturing.
- **Bottom of Funnel (BOFU - Conversion):** ROI calculators, live demos, customer testimonials, targeted sales collateral. Goal: Drive the final purchase decision.

## Content Operations and Distribution

### Content Calendar Planning
- Use a centralized editorial calendar (e.g., Notion, Asana) to track production status, publish dates, assigned owners, and target keywords.
- Plan "Hero" content (large quarterly reports/events), "Hub" content (regular weekly blogs/podcasts), and "Hygiene" content (daily social posts, evergreen SEO updates).

### Content Repurposing Engine
Never create a piece of content for a single use.
- **The Process:** One heavy piece of content (e.g., a 45-minute webinar) should be chopped down into: 1 long-form blog, 3 short YouTube clips, 5 LinkedIn text posts, 1 infographic, and 1 email newsletter.

## Measurement and Optimization

### Key Performance Metrics
- **Consumption:** Pageviews, video watch time, podcast downloads.
- **Engagement:** Likes, comments, shares, time on page, scroll depth.
- **Conversion:** Lead capture rate, newsletter signups, click-through rate on CTA.
- **SEO/Authority:** Backlinks earned, keyword rankings, domain authority.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Publishing Without Distribution:** Spending 80% of the time creating content and 20% distributing it. The ratio should be reversed.
2. **Ignoring the CTA:** Every piece of content must tell the user what to do next (e.g., "Read the full guide," "Subscribe," "Book a demo").
3. **Generic Corporate Speak:** Lack of a distinct brand voice makes content forgettable. Authenticity and opinionated takes perform better than sterile corporate messaging.

## Actionable Recommendations
- Audit your existing content library annually. Update outdated high-performing posts, merge duplicate topics, and delete/redirect low-value pages.
- Implement personalization on your website. Show different content recommendations based on the user's industry or previous browsing behavior.
- Use social listening tools to identify what questions your audience is asking, and build your content calendar around answering them.
