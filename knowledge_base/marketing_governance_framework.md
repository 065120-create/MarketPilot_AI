# Marketing Governance Framework

## Overview
Marketing Governance establishes the rules, processes, and controls that ensure marketing activities are legally compliant, on-brand, financially responsible, and aligned with corporate strategy. Strong governance minimizes risk while empowering teams to execute efficiently.

## Brand and Quality Governance

### Brand Guidelines Compliance
Maintaining a consistent brand identity across all touchpoints is crucial for building trust and recognition.
- **The Guidelines:** Should clearly define logo usage, typography, color palettes (HEX/RGB/CMYK), brand voice/tone, and imagery style.
- **Governance Process:** Require creative assets to pass through a centralized digital asset management (DAM) system or a brand review committee before deployment.

### Quality Assurance (QA) Checklists
Preventing embarrassing or costly errors in public-facing campaigns.
- **Pre-Launch QA:**
  - Broken link checks.
  - Mobile responsiveness testing.
  - Grammar and spelling reviews.
  - Cross-browser compatibility.
  - Accessibility checks (e.g., ALT text for screen readers).

## Legal, Regulatory, and Privacy Compliance

### Data Privacy (GDPR, CCPA)
Marketing teams handle massive amounts of personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Consent:** Ensure explicit opt-in for cookies and email marketing where required by law (e.g., GDPR in Europe).
- **Data Subject Rights:** Implement processes to handle requests for data deletion or data access within legally mandated timeframes.
- **Third-Party Data:** Audit agencies and vendors to ensure they comply with privacy standards. Never buy unsolicited email lists.

### Advertising Standards and Disclosures
- **Truth in Advertising:** Ensure claims are substantiated. Avoid deceptive pricing or false scarcity.
- **Influencer Disclosures:** Mandate that influencers clearly disclose paid partnerships (e.g., using #ad or native platform tools) in compliance with FTC guidelines.

## Financial and Operational Governance

### Budget Governance and Approval Workflows
- **Approval Matrix:** Define who can approve spend based on thresholds (e.g., Manager approves <$5k, Director <$50k, CMO >$50k).
- **PO Process:** Require Purchase Orders before vendor work begins to track committed vs. actual spend.
- **Performance Thresholds:** Establish automated pause rules in ad accounts (e.g., if CPA exceeds $200 for 48 hours, automatically pause the campaign pending review).

### Escalation Procedures and Crisis Communication
- **Escalation Path:** Define a clear chain of command if a campaign goes wrong (e.g., offensive ad goes viral, data breach occurs).
- **Crisis Playbook:** Maintain pre-approved holding statements for PR, protocols for pausing all automated outbound marketing during a crisis, and designated spokespeople.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Over-Governance:** Creating so much red tape that it takes 4 weeks to approve a single tweet, destroying marketing agility.
2. **"Set It and Forget It" Compliance:** Assuming a privacy policy drafted 5 years ago still covers new martech tools or new state regulations.
3. **Shadow IT/Marketing:** Teams bypassing official channels to buy unapproved software or run rogue campaigns, creating massive security and brand risks.

## Actionable Recommendations
- Establish a cross-functional marketing governance board (Marketing, Legal, IT, Finance) that meets quarterly to review policies and major risks.
- Automate compliance where possible (e.g., using tools like OneTrust for cookie consent, or grammar-checking software embedded in workflows).
- Conduct bi-annual training for the marketing team on data privacy, security, and brand standards. Ensure onboarding for new hires includes a governance deep-dive.
